FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Who is Spider-Man?

Spider-Man is a fictional superhero created by writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko. He first appeared in Amazing Fantasy #15 in 1962.

What are Spider-Man's powers?

Spider-Man has superhuman strength, agility, and the ability to cling to walls. He also has a "spider-sense" that alerts him to danger.

Who are Spider-Man's main villains?

Some of Spider-Man's main villains include the Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, Venom, and the Sandman.

What is Spider-Man's real name?

Spider-Man's real name is Peter Parker. He is a high school student who gains his powers after being bitten by a radioactive spider.
